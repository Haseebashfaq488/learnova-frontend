Topic: Circular Motion & Centripetal Acceleration

A 1,200 kg car rounds a flat curve of radius 50 meters at a constant speed of 15 m/s. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction required between the tires and the road to prevent slipping?

Assume acceleration due to gravity is \(g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2\).

auto_awesome
AI Tutor Micro-Hint

lightbulb Strategic Hint:

Remember that for a car on an unbanked flat turn, the centripetal acceleration is supplied entirely by the static friction force: f_s = m × (v² / r) ≤ μ_s × m × g Notice how the car's mass \(m\) cancels out completely on both sides!
